# Design: Sellable UPsych — trust first, then a coverage-first UI

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-26
Branch: main
Repo: modidiviyansh/PsychE
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup

## Problem Statement
The founder wants UPsych (PsychE, v6.6.0) to become a product schools buy. Their stated problem was "its UI/UX." The audit and session found three layers, in the order they kill a sale:
1. **Trust.** Login is a 4-digit PIN checked in the browser, with fallback PINs hard-coded in the shipped bundle (`src/components/PinScreen.tsx:14`) and a `localStorage` "verified" flag. Supabase RLS grants the anonymous role read/write on student and counselling-log tables (`fix_rls.sql`). The anon key ships in the bundle, so minors' counselling records are reachable without logging in.
2. **Single-school identity.** "UPsych : GCM Edition" / "GCM Convent School" is hard-coded in 14 places, including printed reports.
3. **The UI hides the moat.** 7 unlabelled header icons, 1,165 inline style blocks, 51 native `alert()`/`confirm()`, dark-only theme with failing contrast, and the assessment/scoring engine (the reason GCM switched) sits behind an unlabelled icon.

## Demand Evidence
- One school (GCM Convent School) uses UPsych daily, unpaid. Stage: has users, not paying.
- GCM switched away from the counselling module inside its paid school ERP because of **assessments + scoring**: the ERP stores only text notes.
- No payment yet, and no second school in conversation. Demand from a paying buyer is unproven (see Assignment).

## Status Quo
A counselling tab inside the school's existing ERP. It is already paid for, so it costs the school nothing extra. UPsych has to win on what the ERP cannot do (measurement, coverage, risk), not on looks.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **User:** the school counsellor (one per school, ~400 students on roll at GCM). **Buyer:** the principal.
- **Wedge (agreed):** class-wide **screening + risk report**. Run an assessment across a class, auto-score, show who is at risk and who has never been seen, and export a report the principal reads.
- **Observed counsellor behaviour (founder, in their words):** "they miss the difference between logging a full detailed counselling session & quick questions inputs where data & other things are generated"; "they fail to use kanban"; "they fail to keep focus on the students who never attended a session."

## Constraints
- Minors' mental-health data, India DPDP Act 2023. Trust must precede polish.
- Live school (GCM) on the production Supabase project. Auth and RLS cutover uses a short after-hours maintenance window (founder-accepted), not zero-downtime.
- Stack stays React 19 + Vite + Supabase. Deployed via Coolify from GitHub `main`.
- **Selling model: one clone per school (founder decision, 2026-10-28).** Each school gets its own Supabase project and its own Coolify deployment, created from GCM's schema (`schema/install.sql`) and configured through that clone's Settings. No shared multi-school database, so no `school_id` now. It gets added only if shared hosting is ever needed (D1 deferral).

## Premises
1. **Trust first (revised after second opinion).** Phase 1 = per-counsellor login with counsellor and principal roles, anon access removed from RLS, school name/logo/accent from Settings. UI polish starts on that base.
2. **Wedge-first polish.** The screening path (run class screening → results/risk list → student report → principal view) and Home get bespoke design. Other screens get the shared design system only.
3. **Split logging by intent.** One entry point, explicit choices: Quick check-in (generates scores) vs Full session (formal record), plus Class screening and Teacher referral.
4. **Coverage on Home.** Home leads with coverage: on roll, screened, never seen, at risk, improved.
5. **Kanban stays, wrong shape (founder rejected "retire it").** Scheduling, capacity and overdue stay. Their contents also surface on Home (Today / This week's plan), and the board remains as the Schedule tab.

## Cross-Model Perspective
Codex was unavailable (auth token revoked); a fresh Claude subagent gave the cold read.
- **Steelman:** UPsych is a measurement layer for school wellbeing. Screen every student, score them on a standard instrument, and show the principal who is at risk and who has never been seen, with a defensible report.
- **Most revealing answer:** "they fail to keep focus on the students who never attended a session." Sell the denominator (every student accounted for). The Kanban and the detailed log both assume students arrive on their own.
- **Challenged premise:** wedge-first polish before trust is a "liability demo." The founder accepted this, so premise 1 now puts trust first.
- **Prototype suggestion:** auth + RLS in ~10h (the subagent's prototype guess; real sizing belongs to eng review), then the screening path, then a coverage strip, then seed a demo school. Its Tailwind suggestion was adopted (D2). Its "skip Kanban" was not (premise 5).

## Approaches Considered
- **A · Extend current CSS + hand-built primitives.** No new dependencies, but slower and riskier for accessibility. Declined.
- **B · Tailwind + shadcn/ui (Radix), migrated gradually, screen by screen.** **Chosen (D2).**
- **C · Two role-based surfaces (Counsellor workspace + Principal portal).** Declined for now. The principal role from Phase 1 keeps the door open.

## Recommended Approach
**Phase 1: Trust (blocks everything else).**

The anon lockdown is urgent. It ships immediately and does not wait for the Assignment.

- **Accounts (invite-only).**
  - Supabase Auth with email + password. Google Workspace sign-in comes later.
  - Public sign-up is disabled in Supabase Auth settings.
  - A school admin invites staff. The founder is GCM's admin.
  - A new `PsychE_Staff` table holds `user_id`, `role` (`counsellor` | `principal` | `admin`) and `active`. There is one school per database, so no `school_id`.
  - Offboarding: set `active = false` and revoke the user's sessions in Supabase Auth. RLS ignores inactive staff.
- **Roles and data access (committed).**
  - `counsellor`: read/write students, logs, assessments and notes.
  - `principal`: deny-by-default on individual student tables. Aggregates come from `SECURITY DEFINER` Postgres functions that return counts only (coverage, journey counts, class patterns, outcomes), never row data. Each function checks that the caller is an active principal or admin in `PsychE_Staff`, and `EXECUTE` is revoked from `anon`. The one exception is acute alerts, below. Individual-record access for principals is a later, explicit decision.
  - `admin`: settings, staff and branding, plus everything a counsellor can do. The founder holds this role at GCM.
- **Clone-ready.**
  - Nothing school-specific lives in code.
  - A new school is: a new Supabase project from `schema/install.sql`, a new Coolify deployment with that project's env vars, and the school's name, logo and accent set in its Settings.
  - The demo school is exactly such a clone.
- **Branding before login.**
  - School name, logo URL and accent live in that clone's single `PsychE_Settings` row.
  - A narrow `public_branding` view exposes only those three fields from the one row (so there are no other schools to list), and only that view is granted to `anon`.
  - Each deployment talks to its own Supabase project via env vars, so the login screen always shows that clone's school.
  - Logos go in a public-read Supabase Storage bucket `branding`. Only admins can write to it.
  - All 14 hard-coded school names, reports included, read from the school row.
- **Attribution.**
  - Add `created_by` and `updated_by` (staff user id) to logs, notes, assessments and students.
  - A `PsychE_Audit` table records who viewed, created, edited or exported each student record.
  - Records created before cutover are labelled "pre-auth (shared PIN)". They are never attributed to a person retroactively.
- **Cutover for GCM (maintenance window, after school hours, ~1 hour).**
  1. Create staff accounts and send invites ahead of time. Counsellors set passwords before the window.
  2. Deploy the auth-enabled frontend behind a flag. The PIN still works.
  3. In the window: run the migration (new staff and audit tables, attribution columns, new RLS that denies `anon`). Flip the flag. Remove the PIN code.
  4. Verify: an anon-key REST call returns zero rows from every PsychE table, `public_branding` returns one row, and counsellors can log in.
  - Rollback: re-apply the previous RLS migration and revert the frontend deploy. No data is deleted at any step, so rollback is lossless.

**Phase 2: Design system foundation.**
Tailwind + shadcn/ui, with the Tailwind reset scoped so existing screens don't break. Tokens are taken from the locked v3 direction. Replace native `alert`/`confirm` with Dialog/Toast. Add a light theme with AA contrast and labelled navigation (Home, Students, Screening, Schedule, Reports).

**Phase 3: Wedge screens on the v3 direction.**

Committed:
- **Home** = coverage strip (on roll, screened %, never seen, improved this month) plus the Student Journey: Never seen → Screened → Flagged → In support → Follow-up → Closed. Each stage has a count, context and its next best action.
- **Log dock** with Quick check-in, Full session and Class screening.
- **Class screening flow** (with consent and acute-risk handling, below).
- **Student report and principal report.**

Provisional, pending /plan-ceo-review scope decisions (shown in v3, not yet committed):
- "This week's plan" (ranked actions)
- "Patterns worth acting on"
- Teacher referral in the dock
- Re-screen and outcome loop

**Definitions (used everywhere: Home, Journey, reports, success criteria).**
Every student on the current academic-year roll sits in exactly one stage. The first matching rule wins, so the stage counts always sum to the roll:
1. **Closed:** their support plan was closed this year and nothing has reopened it.
2. **Follow-up:** a scheduled follow-up is due or overdue.
3. **In support:** they have an open support plan or a session this year, and no follow-up is due. This includes students seen in sessions who were never screened.
4. **Flagged:** their latest screening crosses the module's risk threshold, or a counsellor flagged them manually, and they are not yet in support.
5. **Screened:** they have a completed screening this year and none of the above apply.
6. **Never seen:** no completed screening and no counselling session of any type this academic year.

**Consent for screening.**
- Consent comes from the school admission form (founder decision, 2026-10-28). GCM's form already covers it.
- One small safeguard: if a parent withdraws consent, the student is marked `opted_out`. That change is logged in `PsychE_Audit`, and the student is excluded from screening and listed as excluded.
- Onboarding a new clone includes confirming that school's admission form carries an equivalent clause.

**Acute-risk escalation (required behaviour).**
- Screening modules mark critical items in the Library, for example self-harm or harm-to-others items.
- Any critical response triggers an alert to the student's counsellor in the app and by email, the moment it is recorded. It also adds a mandatory same-school-day action to the plan, which cannot be dismissed without a logged outcome.
- Coverage for when the counsellor isn't there:
  - If the assigned counsellor is inactive or marked on leave, the alert goes straight to the escalation contact.
  - If the counsellor hasn't acknowledged it within 2 school hours, the escalation contact gets an email too.
  - If the action is still open at the end of the school day, both get a reminder.
- The escalation contact is the principal by default; the admin can change it. This is the one exception to aggregates-only: the contact sees the student's name, class and that an acute alert is open, so they can act. They do not see answers or scores.
- Parents are never auto-contacted; the counsellor does that following the school's protocol.

**Instruments (gate).**
- Class screening reuses the existing assessment engine and Library modules (`PsychE_Modules`, 7-domain scoring).
- Before class screening ships, at GCM too, the screening module(s) must be confirmed as validated for ages 11-18, with licensing cleared and risk and critical-item thresholds documented in `docs/ARCH_technical-specs.md` §8.

**Principal report.** An in-app view plus an A4 PDF/print export, reusing `src/components/print/*`. It contains aggregates only, per the principal role.

**Visual north star:** `docs/designs/assets/direction-v3.png` (source: `direction-v3.html`).
- Brand layer: illustrated hero, flat folk style with ink outlines.
- Work layer: bold flat colour blocks, 2px ink outlines, hard offset shadows.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for display, Inter for UI.

**Content guardrail:** playful frame, calm content. No emoji, stickers or jokes on anything about a specific child's difficulty. Celebration only at school level (coverage, improvement, closure).

Explorations that were rejected (kept for reference only):
- `direction.png`, `direction-v2.png`: too soft.
- `realm-a-storybook.png`, `realm-b-scrapbook.png`, `realm-c-toybox.png`: the founder preferred v3.

## Open Questions
- Scope of the provisional Phase 3 items (ranked weekly plan, class patterns, teacher referral, re-screen/outcome loop, parent-meeting consent tracking). Decided in /plan-ceo-review.
- Whether principals ever get individual-record access, and under what safeguards. Phase 1 ships aggregates-only.
- Pricing unit: per school per year, or per student. Unknown until the Assignment is done.

## Success Criteria
- Zero anonymous data access, verified on production: an anon-key REST call returns zero rows from every PsychE table. The only anon-readable surface is the `public_branding` view (name, logo URL, accent).
- Public sign-up is disabled, and an uninvited account sees nothing.
- No PIN code in the shipped bundle.
- Every post-cutover create, edit and export has `created_by`/`updated_by` and a `PsychE_Audit` row. Pre-cutover rows show "pre-auth (shared PIN)".
- No hard-coded school name anywhere. A demo clone (its own Supabase project and deployment) renders with its own name and logo, using only Settings changes.
- Screening never includes an `opted_out` student. Every critical-item response produces an alert and a same-day action, and reaches the escalation contact if it isn't acknowledged in time.
- Journey stage counts always sum to the roll.
- GCM counsellor test: in an observed session, they pick the right log type without help, and they can name how many students are never seen.
- A principal receives a class screening report and says they would pay for it (Assignment).

## Distribution Plan
Existing pipeline: Coolify auto-deploys the frontend from GitHub `main`. Database changes ship as numbered SQL migrations (current pattern `vNN_*.sql`) plus `schema/install.sql`.

## Dependencies
- Phase 1 blocks Phases 2 and 3.
- Needs access to the production Supabase project (Auth settings, RLS migration) and a GCM maintenance window.
- Codex outside review needs `codex login`.

## The Assignment

As the authorised GCM admin, pull GCM's real coverage numbers from production as aggregate counts only (no names): never seen, flagged, and screened by class. Take them to GCM's principal as a one-page screening summary. Ask two questions: "What would you pay per term to have this for every class?" and "Which principal should I show this to next?" Write down the exact words of both answers.

### R2-1 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> The Recommended Approach now commits to a ~1 hour after-hours maintenance window, but Constraints still says auth and RLS changes 'need a no-downtime cutover'. The R1-1 contradiction between Constraints and the cutover plan remains.

**Remedy**

> Update the Constraints line to match the chosen maintenance-window cutover, or change the cutover so it is zero-downtime.

### R2-2 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> Principals are denied individual student rows but read aggregate views. In Postgres/Supabase a view either runs with the caller's RLS (so a principal denied every row gets zero counts) or runs as its owner and bypasses RLS (so unless the view filters by the caller's school_id, it leaks other schools' aggregates). The design does not say how aggregate access is enforced, and the cross-school isolation success criterion depends on it.

**Remedy**

> Specify how aggregates are produced, for example security-definer functions or views that filter on the caller's active PsychE_Staff school_id and role. State that they never return row-level data, and add a test showing a school #2 principal cannot get GCM aggregates.

### R2-3 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> The escalation contact defaults to the principal, whose committed role is deny-by-default on individual student records. The design does not say whether the acute-alert notice identifies the student. If it does, that conflicts with aggregates-only. If it does not, the principal cannot act on it.

**Remedy**

> State exactly what the notice contains (student identity or not) and reconcile it with the principal role, for example as an explicit, audited exception for acute alerts.

### R2-4 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> Acute-risk alerts go only in-app to 'the student's counsellor'. Nothing covers the counsellor being logged out, absent, inactive, or a school with one counsellor on leave. Nothing says who sees the alert if the same-school-day action is missed, or whether any out-of-band channel (email/SMS) is used. For a self-harm signal in a minor, an alert nobody sees is a safety risk.

**Remedy**

> Define the delivery channel(s), a fallback recipient when the counsellor is unavailable or inactive, and what happens when the same-day action is overdue (for example, escalate to the escalation contact).

### R2-5 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> With several schools in one database, the pre-login screen has to know which school's branding to show, but the design never says how it picks one (subdomain, URL path, per-deployment env var). The success criterion that school #2 'renders with its own name and logo' depends on this. As written, the view also lets anyone list every school using the product.

**Remedy**

> Specify how the pre-login screen resolves the school (and therefore which public_branding row it reads), and whether anon can list all schools or only look one up by key.
